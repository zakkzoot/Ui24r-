# Apartment Renovation — Maximum-ROI Refresh

**Brief:** Lift the rental value of this Phnom Penh apartment **without major
construction** (minor works only), spending as little as possible for the
largest rent uplift. This is a *staging-grade* refresh — paint, soft
furnishings, lighting, greenery and styling — not a remodel.

> **Deliverables in this folder**
> - `moodboard/moodboard.png` — colour palette, materials, strategy & budget on one board
> - `renders/compare_*.jpg` — labelled **before / after** boards for 4 rooms
> - `renders/reno_*.jpg` — the restyled "after" room images

## The core problem

The apartment has good *bones* — high ceilings, an open plan, big windows, a
granite kitchen bar and an already-renovated bathroom. What drags the
photos (and the perceived value) down is **visual chaos**: bright-red
curtains, lime-green chairs, an orange papasan, a multicolour runner and
yellow bedding all fighting each other against cold grey tile. It reads as
*cheap and dated*, not *calm and premium*.

**Fix the palette, not the structure.** Almost all the uplift here is free
or near-free: declutter, repaint scuffs, and swap loud soft-furnishings for a
cohesive neutral scheme with **one** accent (the navy sofas, which are
timeless and stay).

## Design direction

| | |
|---|---|
| **Look** | Warm minimal / "quiet luxury" — neutral base, natural textures |
| **Base** | Warm white walls, soft-linen curtains, greige upholstery |
| **Accent** | Navy (existing sofas) + black metal (existing legs/frames) |
| **Texture** | Jute rug, rattan, light wood, indoor plants, brushed brass |
| **Lighting** | Warm-white 2700K bulbs everywhere (kills the clinical feel) |

Full palette with hex codes is on `moodboard/moodboard.png`.

## Room-by-room

### Living / open-plan lounge
- Replace **red curtains → oat/ivory linen** (single biggest visual win).
- **Slipcover the lime chairs in greige** cotton (≈$15–25 each, washable) — or
  replace covers; keep the frames.
- Swap the busy runner for a plain **taupe/charcoal** one; style the bar with
  a stool cushion, a bowl and a small plant.
- Add a **jute rug** under the coffee table to warm the grey tile and anchor
  the seating.
- A **tall plant** (palm / monstera) in the empty corner; a couple of framed
  prints on the big blank walls.
- Re-style the papasan with a neutral cushion instead of red/orange.

### Master bedroom
- **Hotel-white bedding** (white duvet + a charcoal throw + 2 cushions)
  instead of yellow — instantly reads "boutique".
- **Soft-linen curtains** in place of the red ones.
- Small rug beside the bed; one piece of art above the headboard wall.

### Kitchen & bar
- Already strong (granite, pendant lights). Just **style** it: bar-stool
  cushions, a fruit bowl, a small herb plant, declutter the worktop.
- Optional peel-and-stick backsplash behind the sink (~$30) if budget allows.

### Bathroom
- Already renovated and on-trend (grey stone, rain shower). **Leave it.**
  Just add white towels, a bath mat and a small plant for the photos.

### Balcony
- High-value, under-sold. A bistro set + 2–3 potted plants turns it into an
  "outdoor space" selling point for almost nothing.

## Budget & ROI

Indicative costs in USD (Phnom Penh local sourcing; furnish-grade, not luxury):

| Item | Spend |
|---|---:|
| Curtains (linen) ×3–4 windows | $90 |
| Chair slipcovers / re-covers | $80 |
| Cushions, throws, bedding (hotel white) | $110 |
| Jute rug + bedside rug | $70 |
| Indoor plants ×4–5 + pots | $70 |
| Warm-white LED bulbs (whole unit) | $30 |
| Paint touch-ups + sundries | $60 |
| Framed art / mirrors | $90 |
| Balcony bistro set | $70 |
| **Total** | **≈ $670** |

**Why the ROI is high**
- Spend is **one-off, mostly portable** (moves to the next unit if needed).
- No permits, no trades, no downtime — can be done in a **weekend**.
- A cohesive, bright, "move-in-ready" look supports a higher asking rent and,
  just as importantly, **lets faster** and **photographs far better** — most
  tenants decide from the listing photos.
- Rule of thumb for staging refreshes like this: a **$600–700 outlay**
  commonly supports a **$30–60/month** higher achievable rent and a shorter
  void period. Even at the low end that is a **payback in roughly a year**,
  and pure profit thereafter — plus reduced vacancy.

### Priority order (if budget is staged)
1. **Curtains + bedding + bulbs** (biggest impact per dollar)
2. **Slipcovers + cushions/throws** (kills the colour clash)
3. **Rug + plants** (warmth & life)
4. **Art, balcony, backsplash** (polish)

---

## What we deliberately did *not* do
No wall removal, no re-tiling, no kitchen/bath rebuild, no rewiring — all
high-cost, low-incremental-return for a rental at this level. Every move
above is reversible and tenant-neutral, which is exactly what maximises ROI.
